## 2. Changes
*what you changed and the reasoning per change.*

**Version 1**
- Halden stats received the largest overall stat reduction, since this was the strongest unit in the game. this in the form of significant reduction to HP, ATK, DEF, SPD, and SKL. Cost unchanged.
- Rookwood: Slight reduction to ATK and DEF. Cost unchanged, due to this unit high speed the damage was reduced a bit to make it more balanced.
- Pyraxis received the highest buff, reducing its cost by 2 and getting his defense and HP increased, plus +1 to his SPD
- the other classes received minor adjustments to get their win% closer to 50%

**Version 2**
- With Version 2 I moved away from keeping units tightly grouped around a similar power-to-cost ratio and instead gave individual units more distinct strengths and weaknesses, this creates a more interesting roster of units and encourages different combinations of units for the team.
- I re-balanced the table taking into account that units like the Archer, Battlemage and Cleric have a higher range than the other units which is not being taken into account in the python simulation, which would make them a bit more stronger in the real game

With Version 1.0, we achieved a roster that trends toward a ~50% win rate while maintaining a flatter relationship between win rate and cost. However, there are several factors that the current simulation does not account for, which may cause some classes to stand out more than others in the actual game.

First, Movement and Range are not currently factored into the simulation. These attributes can significantly increase the value of certain units in actual gameplay, potentially causing some units to outperform others despite having similar simulated results.

Additionally, these results are generated by the Python simulation, which determines win rate based on unit-versus-unit fights. In the actual game, however, units do not fight directly against other units. Instead, they fight against specific enemies, meaning their effectiveness will also depend on how their individual strengths and weaknesses interact with the enemy roster.

Version 2.0 aims to introduce more variation in unit costs. In the previous version, I unintentionally kept costs too similar to maintain a consistent WIN%/COST ratio. This version allows for greater cost differentiation while keeping the roster within a WIN%/COST range of approximately 7.5 to 8.3.

This version also accounts for the Range advantage of the Cleric, Archer, and Battlemage, which the duel simulator cannot measure. These units are therefore slightly weaker in the win%/cost of the simulation to compensate for their stronger positioning potential in real encounters.

The Battlemage has also received a power increase alongside a higher cost.
